Important APIs for Drag-n-Drop:

Abstract Window Toolkit
https://docs.oracle.com/javase/8/docs/api/java/awt/package-summary.html

Swing
https://docs.oracle.com/javase/tutorial/uiswing/index.html

* Swing is built on-top of awt and extends their classes, so;
* Don't mix components, use Swing components only when there is a component for both.
      (i.e., use JFrame, not Frame)
* awt is used for event handling and some graphics.
